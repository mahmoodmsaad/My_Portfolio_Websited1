Research Statement

I deploy first-principles simulations and data-driven workflows to explore how low-dimensional materials behave in harsh environments. By coupling density functional theory with machine learning, I uncover design rules for emerging drug delivery platforms, tunable gas sensors, and photovoltaic interfaces that can accelerate clean-energy and health solutions.

Teaching & Mentorship

Through research appointments in Faisalabad and Trieste, I support experimental collaborators, guide peers on computational tooling, and contribute to open learning resources that make advanced simulation techniques more accessible.

Research Focus

Targeted investigations that turn atomistic insights into practical technologies.

Atomistic Drug Delivery Platforms

Modeling phosphorene-based carriers to optimize antiviral drug encapsulation, release kinetics, and biocompatibility with the goal of elevating therapeutic performance.

Gas Sensing Materials

Evaluating graphitic carbon nitride and related 2D lattices for selective nitrogen oxides detection, linking adsorption energetics to measurable spectroscopic signatures.

Photovoltaic Interfaces

Exploring charge-transfer dynamics in hybrid materials, informing the design of stable interfaces that extend device lifetimes and energy yield.
